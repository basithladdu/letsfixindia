Source definitions

Role: Prime Minister's Office portfolio list dated 25 July 2026.

Affidavit: candidate-declared age, education, profession, assets, liabilities and pending-case count published by MyNeta for Lok Sabha 2024. A declared case is not a conviction.

Body data: height, weight and BMI stay unavailable unless both measurements have a cited publication. No estimate is made from an image.

Coverage: this eight-person Cabinet comparison is deliberately finite, not a directory of every office-holder.
